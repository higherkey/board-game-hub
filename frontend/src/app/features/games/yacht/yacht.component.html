How to play Yacht

Take turns rolling five dice up to three times. Tap dice to keep or release them between rolls. Choose one unused category to end your turn; you can score after any roll.

Complete all 13 categories. Earn 35 extra points for an upper subtotal of at least 63. Highest total wins; tied players share the win.

A repeat five of a kind earns 100 extra points only if your Yacht slot already contains 50. You must fill the matching upper slot if it is empty; otherwise choose a lower slot using Joker scores (25 / 30 / 40 for full house / small / large straight). If all lower slots are used, fill another upper slot with zero. A zero in Yacht disables these bonuses.
