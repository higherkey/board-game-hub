How Table vs. Hand Works

A shared living-room experience built for any screen, anywhere.

The Big Screen Table

Put the game up on your living room TV or laptop for everyone to see. It acts as the shared board — showing scores, timers, and round action.

Your Private Hand

Each player joins on their smartphone. Secret cards, answers, and votes stay private on your own screen so no one else can peek.

Instant Multiplayer Fun

Zero downloads or sign-ups required to jump in. Everyone interacts in real time with instant updates across all your screens.
