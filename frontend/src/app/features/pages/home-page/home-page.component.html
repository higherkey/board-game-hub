How It Works

Three steps from zero to playing

01

Host on the Big Screen

Open BoardGameHub on your TV, laptop, or tablet. Pick a game and create a room — you’ll get a unique 4-letter code to share with players.

02

Join on Your Phone

Players visit the site and enter the room code. No app download needed — your phone becomes your private game controller instantly.

03

Play Together, In Sync

The TV shows the shared game board. Each player sees their private hand on their phone. Real-time, synchronized, zero lag.
