How Table vs. Hand Works

The Table Role

1. Host on the Big Screen

Open BoardGameHub on your TV, laptop, or tablet. Create a room to get a unique 4-letter code. This screen acts as the Table — displaying public scores, game boards, timers, and shared moments that everyone watches together.

The Hand Role

2. Join on Your Phone

Players scan the room's QR code or type in the room code on their smartphones. Your phone acts as your Hand — your private, personal controller for entering secret clues, drawing, voting, and making your moves.
