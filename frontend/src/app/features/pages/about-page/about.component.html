Our mission

Distance disappears at the game table

Board Game Hub started with one idea from eight1five design: bring friends and families together across any distance. Across the street or across an ocean, we turn screens into a shared living room.

Two screens, one game

The table

A TV, laptop or tablet everyone can see. It holds the board, the dice, the scores and the seats: everything that is public.

Your hand

Your phone. Your cards, your secret word, your roll. Only you see it, and the buttons sit where your thumb already is.

How a game night works

  1. 1

    Host

    Open Board Game Hub on a TV, laptop or tablet and pick a game off the shelf. That screen becomes the table and shows a 4-letter room code and a QR code. You can change the rules before anyone sits down.

  2. 2

    Join

    Everyone scans the QR code or types the room code on their phone. No app to install and no account needed. Each phone takes a seat at the table, and the game starts when everyone is ready.

  3. 3

    Play

    Your phone is your hand: your cards, your dice, your secret word. The table shows everything everyone should see, like the board, the scores and whose turn it is.

Where you can play

In the same room

Put the table on your TV or laptop. Everyone joins with their phone and keeps their cards, dice and answers private, just like holding a hand of cards.

Miles apart

Every home opens the same table on its own screen, so nobody has to share their screen on a call. Video seats around the table are on the way.
